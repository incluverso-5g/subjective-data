# XR Collaborative Experiment – Questionnaire Description

## Pre-Questionnaire
1. **ID**: Unique identifier for each participant.
2. **User ID**: User identifier (alias or code).
3. **Age**.
4. **Gender**: Male / Female.
5. **Vision**: Normal or corrected.
6. **Education level**.
7. **Experience level using VR headsets**: Basic, Intermediate, etc.
8. **Debate experience level**: Basic, Intermediate, Expert.

---

## Participants
A total of **24 participants** took part in the test, with an average age of **44 ± 11.11** years.  
Of these, **45.8% identified as female** and **54.2% as male**.

---

## Post-Questionnaire

### Experimental Setup
The test included **three technological conditions**, and the experiment was conducted in groups of **three participants**.

In each technological condition:
- **Two participants** joined **remotely** (acting as experts).
- **One participant** joined **locally**, positioned next to the owl device.

Across iterations, the technological condition changed and, in some cases, the assigned role also changed. Due to the experimental design, there were always two remote participants and one local participant, resulting in a larger amount of data collected from the remote experience compared to the local one.

---

### Identification
- **ID**: Please answer with the ID you were provided with.
- **Group ID**: Please answer with the Group ID you were assigned to. Each group consisted of three participants.
- **Tested condition**: The order was randomized; participants recorded the condition they tested and evaluated.

### Simulator Sickness
- Are you feeling any sickness or discomfort right now?

---

### Spatial Presence
- How natural did your interactions with the environment seem?
- How compelling was your sense of objects moving through space?
- How consistent were your experiences in the virtual environment with real-world experiences?
- How quickly did you adjust to the virtual environment?
- How proficient did you feel moving and interacting in the virtual environment at the end of the experience?
- How well could you concentrate on the assigned tasks rather than on the mechanisms used to perform them?
- How involved were you in the virtual environment experience?

---

### Social Presence
- I felt that people were talking to me.
- I felt that I was listening to the others in the video.
- I felt I was present with the other people in the video.
- I felt like the people could see me.
- I felt I was actually interacting with other people.

---

### Cognitive Load
- How mentally demanding was the task?
- How physically demanding was the task?
- How hurried or rushed was the pace of the task?
- How successful were you in accomplishing what you were asked to do?
- How hard did you have to work to achieve your level of performance?
- How insecure, discouraged, irritated, stressed, and annoyed were you?

---

### Quality of Interaction (QoI)
- I felt connected with my partner.
- I felt I was present with my partner.
- My partner could tell when I needed assistance.
- I was able to understand my partner's message.
- My partner and I worked together well.
- I was able to express myself clearly.
- Information from my partner was helpful.
